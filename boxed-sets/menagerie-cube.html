Menagerie Cube

Draft · v0.1 · decisions recorded November 15, 2026 · not yet playtested

A six-player draft cube about mad scientists combining creatures. This page has the cube's rules (MC1 to MC4), the full 270-slot card list, and the design goals and decisions behind it. Every candidate card, including the cut ones, is in the candidate pool and research.

Draft v0.1: complete list, not yet playtested

The cube has a full 270-slot list. Balance is untested, and several rulings are still open (MC2, MC4). The list will change after the first drafts.

The cube

Every player is a mad scientist building monsters. Creatures combine with each other through augment and hosts, mutate, bestow, and the Un-set stuck-together creatures (B.F.M., S.N.O.T., Grusilda). Avatar: The Last Airbender hybrid animals (Tiger-Seal, Platypus-Bear, Cat-Gator) and other non-Human creatures are the bodies the experiments are built on; they already read like Unstable's augment creatures. Noncreature spells are added where an archetype or color needs interaction.

The cube is drafted by 6 players with 3 packs of 15 cards each (270 pick slots), followed by 1v1 matches.

Un-cards follow the Un-cards guideline: cards that bend the game's rules are in; cards that count things in the room or need physical stunts are out.

Base rules and cube changes

The base ruleset is Booster-draft Cube, which covers the draft, deck construction, and games. Decks are at least 40 cards, games are 1v1 and start at 20 life, and normal Magic rules apply. Basic lands come from a separate land station.

This cube adds three rules and has one open rulings sheet:

  • MC1 (addition): bundled picks. Drafting the S.N.O.T. in the packs also takes the rest of the stack from a side pile.
  • MC2 (unresolved): interactions that need a written ruling before the first playtest.
  • MC3 (addition): Crafty Octopus comes with a preset Contraption deck.
  • MC4 (addition): B.F.M. is not drafted; any player may cast it from outside the game.

MC1. Bundled picks

Some cards only work together. Each bundle has one card in the cube's packs. The rest of the bundle waits in a side pile next to the draft. When you draft the pack card, take the rest of its bundle from the side pile and add it to your drafted pool.

Bundle Card in the pack Side pile
S.N.O.T. stack One S.N.O.T. The other copies. Stack size is not decided; four is the working number.

A bundle fills one pick slot, so packs stay at 15 cards. All slot counts on this page count each bundle once.

After the draft, each card in a bundle is an ordinary drafted card. You may play any of them, all of them, or none.

MC2. Rulings sheet: unresolved

These interactions need written rulings before the first playtest. None is decided. Until one is written, the table has no agreed answer for them. Check the Unstable and Unfinity release notes and the Comprehensive Rules first; write a table ruling only where they don't answer the question.

  1. Mutating onto a host that has been augmented, or augmenting a host that has a mutated creature on it.
  2. What returns when a combined or mutated creature is flickered (Ordinary Pony) or exiled and returned.
  3. Whether a combined host and augment has the creature types of both cards. This matters for Diligent Zookeeper.
  4. Whether casting a bestow creature as an Aura counts as "enchant a creature you control" for Surgeon General Commander. The card text reads that way. Confirm it.
  5. Mer Man is a Human Fish, so mutate can't target it. This follows from the card's types; it goes on the sheet as a reminder, not as a house rule.
  6. What counts as an "Un-cost" for Underdome's colored mana.
  7. The undecided B.F.M. questions listed under MC4.

MC3. Crafty Octopus and its Contraption deck

Crafty Octopus is the only card in the cube that assembles a Contraption. Contraptions are not drafted. A preset Contraption deck waits in the side pile with the bundles. When you draft Crafty Octopus, take the Contraption deck too.

The Octopus Contraption deck (10 cards): Genetic Recombinator, Duplication Device, Refibrillator, Pet Project, Neural Network, Bee-Bee Gun, Thud-for-Duds, Oaken Power Suit, Arms Depot, Applied Aeronautics. These were picked because they act on creatures. This proposed list is accepted for now and is not final.

Contraption rules, from Wizards' Unstable mechanics article:

  1. The Contraption deck is separate from your main deck and does not count toward your 40 cards. In Limited, it has no minimum size and no singleton rule.
  2. At the start of the game, shuffle the Contraption deck and put it face down near your library.
  3. You have three sprockets (1, 2, and 3) and a CRANK! counter that marks the current sprocket.
  4. To assemble a Contraption, turn the top card of your Contraption deck face up and put it onto the battlefield on one of your sprockets. Crafty Octopus assembles one when it enters.
  5. At the beginning of your upkeep, if you control a Contraption, move the CRANK! counter to the next sprocket (1, 2, 3, then 1 again). You may then crank any Contraptions on that sprocket, which triggers their abilities. Cranking is optional.
  6. Contraptions are artifacts. A Contraption that would go to a graveyard goes to your scrapyard instead.
  7. If your Contraption deck is empty when you would assemble, nothing happens. You don't lose the game.

MC4. Big Furry Monster

B.F.M. (Big Furry Monster) is not in the draft. It waits beside the play area, and any player may cast it. The owner decided this rule on November 15, 2026.

  1. Casting. Any player may cast B.F.M. from outside the game, any time they could cast a creature spell. Cast it as one spell: both halves together, with the right half's cost of fifteen black mana ({B}{B}{B}{B}{B}{B}{B}{B}{B}{B}{B}{B}{B}{B}{B}).
  2. Sacrifice toward the cost. As you cast B.F.M., you may sacrifice any number of creatures you control. Each creature sacrificed pays {B} of the cost. Pay the rest with mana.
  3. On the battlefield. B.F.M. is one 99/99 creature made of both cards. It can't be blocked except by three or more creatures. If one half leaves the battlefield, sacrifice the other half (printed text).

Not decided yet:

  • What happens to B.F.M. when it leaves the battlefield, and whether it can be cast again in the same game.
  • Whether the casting player must have both halves "in hand" in any sense, or simply takes them from beside the play area.
  • How many copies the box needs. With three 1v1 matches at once, one B.F.M. serves one table; the other tables need their own copy or a stand-in.
  • Whether B.F.M. later ties into a larger cube mechanic.

Superseded options. Two earlier options were replaced by this rule: the owner's reading of the printed card (cast the right half while holding the left half, and attach the left half as it enters; under that reading, returning the right half from the graveyard while holding the left half would also work), and leaving B.F.M. as a printed joke card. Black mana engines (Crypt Ghast and similar) were ruled out: too many cards for one card's sake, with nothing for the theme.

Size targets

The list has 270 draftable slots, which matches the 270-slot target. The targets by section are 42 per color, 20 multicolor, 12 colorless, and 26 lands.

Section Target Candidate pool Change needed
White 42 40 add 2
Blue 42 41 add 1
Black 42 40 add 2
Red 42 42 none
Green 42 44 over by 2; accepted
Multicolor 20 25 over by 5
Colorless 12 12 none
Lands 26 26 none

How the targets were set:

  • Colors. Archetypes don't follow color, but each color still needs a fair share of the draft so packs aren't lopsided. Colors within a few cards of 42 are accepted; green and red running slightly high is expected in a creature cube.
  • Multicolor. Lowered from 40 to 20, because archetypes no longer follow color pairs.
  • Lands. First proposed as 28, now 26. Basic lands come from a separate land station, so cube lands are only fixing.
  • Overall size. A cube can be a little larger than the draft needs; extra cards just go unpicked in a given draft.

How slots are counted: a bundle counts as one slot, in the color of its creature. Pool counts include the interaction candidates, count each bundle once, and count four copies of each Vivid land. The balance snapshot has counts by color, theme, card type, and mana value.

The next step is the owner's keep pass, then adding cards to support the chosen themes.

Gameplay goals

Cuts and additions are judged against these design targets.

  1. Most decks combine creatures. Hosts, mutate targets, and bestow targets are common enough that a drafter who wants to combine can.
  2. Non-Human bodies. Mutate can only target non-Humans. Humans are kept rare. The few left (Animal Attendant, Diligent Zookeeper, Mer Man) fit the theme and stay.
  3. Slower than a usual cube, without stalling. Everyone's fixing enters tapped, which gives combined creatures time to come together. Two- and three-drops are very common, so aggressive decks can punish a slow start, with some expensive creatures to save mana for.
  4. Archetypes are mechanics, not colors. Bestow, mutate, and hosts appear in every color, so a deck is defined by what it combines, not by its color pair. Four copies of each Vivid land make it practical to play the cards you want from any colors.
  5. Removal is fair to stacking, and on theme. A combined creature holds two or more cards, so one destroy spell trades for all of them. Most removal is bites, fights, edicts, Auras, damage, or turning a creature into a small animal (Turn to Frog, Rapid Hybridization). No counterspells. Prefer interaction where animals and monsters fight each other, especially "your creature deals damage to another creature." Generic removal from other themes is a stray, even if it plays fine.
  6. Players can fight over big creatures without only attacking. When someone builds a huge combined creature, the opponent needs ways to answer or contest it: bites, fights, edicts, theft (Hammer Helper), and protection for their own creature (Keep Safe, Success!, Fight as One, ward).
  7. Interaction is at least 20% of the nonland cards. This is a creature-heavy cube, so it runs lower than most cubes, but not lower than this. Weight new interaction toward blue, red, white, and green.
  8. Augment needs bodies. Augments do nothing alone, so hosts matter more than augments. Augment is the weakest core theme because augments can't go on ordinary animals; it gets repeat copies and extra support.
  9. No unsupported side themes. Cards that need a partner theme the cube doesn't have are cut: Lessons, Shrines, Allies, Bison, Spirits, "draw your second card," and "legendary matters." A hybrid animal stays unless its ability does almost nothing here; being a little weaker is fine.
  10. Bombs are allowed for now. Cards that win alone if unanswered stay in for the first playtests. Revisit after playing.
  11. Mad science and zoological disaster, not eldritch horror. Mechanics that combine creatures but read as Eldrazi or nightmare (emerge) are out. Bestow creatures must be animals or mythic hybrids (Satyr, Minotaur, Siren, Manticore, Hydra, Unicorn), not Spirits, Nymphs, Demons, or Merfolk.
  12. No ramp package. The format is meant to be slow, and Vivid lands handle fixing. Animal Attendant is the only mana creature left.
  13. Colors stay roughly even. Archetypes don't follow color, but every mechanic appears in every color, so a color with far more cards than the others would crowd the packs.

Archetypes

The archetypes are not decided. Bestow, mutate, and hosts each appear in every color, so archetypes will be defined by mechanic, not by color pair.

The plan for choosing them:

  1. Cut the pool down.
  2. The owner marks the cards they most want to keep.
  3. Pick themes from what those cards already do.
  4. Add cards to support those themes.

Proposed core: what Surgeon General Commander cares about

The owner proposed this core on November 15, 2026. It is under discussion and not final.

Surgeon General Commander draws a card whenever you augment, enchant, or mutate a creature you control, and taps for any color. It fits any deck and is the draft's chase card. Its three triggers are the three proposed core archetypes:

Archetype What the deck does Cards in the pool
Augment Play hosts, then combine augments onto them. 14 augments, 20 hosts, 5 support cards (Clever Combo, Teacher's Pet, Dr. Julius Jumblemorph, Labro Bot, Really Epic Punch, Success!)
Mutate Stack mutate creatures onto non-Human creatures. 34 mutate creatures, 6 support cards
Bestow Enchant your creatures with bestow creatures. The goal is enchanting creatures, not an enchantment deck. 40 bestow creatures

Hybrid animals are the shared bodies: non-Human creatures to mutate onto and bestow onto. Hosts are a separate card type; augments can only combine with hosts.

Other build-arounds: Dr. Julius Jumblemorph (finds augments), Grusilda, Monster Masher (combined and enchanted creatures get menace; combines creatures from graveyards), Diligent Zookeeper (rewards many creature types), Morophon, and Chromanticore.

Decisions so far

All of these were recorded on November 15, 2026.

Topic Decision
Player count 6 drafters: 3 packs of 15, so 270 pick slots. Changed from 8 players to keep the cube smaller and easier to assemble.
Singleton One copy of each card, except the S.N.O.T. stack and the Vivid lands. Augment may need repeat copies: it is the slimmest core theme, and every augment card ever printed is already in the pool.
Games 1v1 matches after the draft.
Base ruleset Booster-draft Cube.
Noncreature spells Add themed interaction and support, then cut back to 270. Cuts are discussed with the owner one group at a time.
Champion Cut entirely. It fit the hybrid-creature idea but isn't mad science.
Companions Cut entirely.
Emerge Cut entirely. It combines creatures, but reads as eldritch nightmare, not mad science.
Archetypes Defined by mechanic, not color pair. Cut first, then pick themes from the cards the owner most wants to keep, then add cards to support those themes.
Bundled picks The S.N.O.T. stack fills one pick slot. One card of each bundle is in the packs; the rest waits in a side pile and is taken when the pack card is drafted. See MC1.
Fixing Four copies of each Vivid land (20 lands). They enter tapped; if everyone's fixing is slow, nobody is behind. Silly lands are welcome as extra fixing: Underdome, Nearby Planet, City of Ass.
Lands cut Original dual lands, shock lands, and Triomes.
Avatar cards Hybrid animals only. Named characters are cut. The Lion-Turtle was kept as a hybrid animal, then cut because it can't attack or block without Lessons. Spirits and Bison Whistle are cut. Animal Attendant and Diligent Zookeeper stay as Human support. Platypus-Bear stays.
Plain animals Giant Fly, Giant Koi, and Gilacorn are cut for now. Plain animals may return if the cube needs padding later.
Mana creatures Cut, except Animal Attendant.
Meld Cut entirely. Five pairs isn't enough for a theme, and meld isn't one of Surgeon General Commander's triggers.
Tentative cuts Nyxborn Shieldmate, Sightless Brawler, Detective's Phoenix. They may come back.
Bestow Bestow creatures that aren't animals or mythic hybrids are cut (21 cards).
B.F.M. Not drafted. Any player may cast it from outside the game by sacrificing creatures toward its cost. See MC4.
Counterspells None. Counterspells are creature hate in spirit.
Contraption deck The proposed ten-card deck in MC3 is accepted.
Contraptions Not drafted. Crafty Octopus comes with a preset Contraption deck. See MC3.

In the order the changes were made:

  • Decisions recorded, archetype plan drafted, candidate pool imported from the owner's Archidekt idea list.
  • Changed to six players.
  • Added 50 interaction candidates.
  • Cut champion, companions, shock lands, Triomes, and named Avatar characters.
  • Set Vivid lands to four copies each.
  • Added gameplay goals.
  • Cut emerge, Avatar Spirits, Bison Whistle, and Serpent of the Pass.
  • Replaced the first-draft color-pair archetype table with a mechanic-based plan, because each mechanic appears in every color.
  • Cut Avatar interaction that wasn't on theme (Lessons, bending, and generic removal).
  • Cut Cloudshift and Descendants' Path.
  • Cut plain animals, Koma, Mirrorwing Dragon, most mana creatures, Urza and Fang + Vanille meld pairs, Duh, and other off-theme cards; changed bundled picks to use a side pile.
  • Cut generic interaction (counterspells, bounce, plain burn and removal), Chains of Custody, Careless Celebrant, Heartless Act, Ruthless Deathfang, the two hybrid changelings, Eladamri's Call, and Emergent Ultimatum.
  • Recorded the owner's proposed core of augment, mutate, and bestow.
  • Cut meld.
  • Cut Just Desserts, Super-Duper Death Ray, and Well Done.
  • Cut non-animal bestow creatures.
  • Added MC3 for Crafty Octopus's Contraption deck.
  • Set interaction to at least 20% of nonland cards.
  • Lowered the multicolor target to 20 and raised each color to 42.
  • Proposed a 79-slot shortlist (Ikoria bodies, Chimeras and Manticores, creature-to-animal interaction, protection, and augment repeats).
  • Added B.F.M. proposals.
  • Approved the shortlist, including repeat copies of augment cards.
  • Cut Disciple of Freyalise, Kazandu Mammoth, Tangled Florahedron, and Beastmaster Ascension; kept Frog-Squirrels, Hippo-Cows, and Saber-Tooth Moose-Lion.
  • Added Savai Sabertooth.
  • Took B.F.M. out of the draft with house rule MC4.
  • Added six fight spells.
  • Cut seven non-animal creature-lands, Birds of Paradise, Dryad Arbor, and Mount Velus Manticore; kept Akoum Warrior and Glasspool Mimic.
  • Added Typhoid Rats for black.
  • Lowered the land target to 26.
  • The pool reaches 270 slots.

Open decisions

  • Archetypes. Is the owner's proposed core of augment, mutate, and bestow adopted? See Archetypes.
  • Rulings for MC2. The interactions listed in MC2 need written rulings before the first playtest.
  • B.F.M. Four questions are undecided under MC4: what happens when it leaves the battlefield and whether it can be cast again, whether the caster needs both halves "in hand" in any sense, how many copies the box needs, and whether it later ties into a larger cube mechanic.
  • S.N.O.T. stack size. Four is the working number.
  • Augment. How many repeat copies, and what support to add? Augment can only combine with hosts, not with ordinary animals.
  • Pack construction. Random packs, or seeded packs with a set number of hosts and augments per pack? There are 14 augments and 20 hosts in the pool, so random packs will sometimes split them badly.
  • Hammer Helper. Kept for now as theft against terrifying creatures. Revisit as the cube fills out.
  • Bombs. Cards that win alone if unanswered stay in for the first playtests. Revisit after playing.
  • Which cards to cut to reach 270. The list now stands at 270 slots, but multicolor is over its target and white, blue, and black are under theirs; see Size targets.
  • Contraption deck. The ten-card list in MC3 is accepted for now and not final.
  • Possible returns. The tentative cuts (Nyxborn Shieldmate, Sightless Brawler, Detective's Phoenix) may come back, and plain animals may return if the cube needs padding.
