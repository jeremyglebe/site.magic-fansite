Challenge Decks

Challenge Decks were Wizards of the Coast's official player-versus-environment products for the Theros block (2013–2014). Each is a 60-card deck that runs itself under its own rules sheet, with special card backs. You play a normal Magic deck against it, alone or with friends. Each deck was used at its set's Game Day and was also sold as a boxed product with a paper copy of the rules playmat.

Each deck has its own rules. Pick one page.

Challenge Set Enemy Players How it plays
Face the Hydra Theros (2013) A Hydra of 0-power Heads 1+ The Hydra never attacks: each untapped Head burns you every turn, and killing a Head can grow two more. Win when no Heads remain at the end of a turn.
Battle the Horde Born of the Gods (2014) A Minotaur horde 1–4 Two cards a turn plus one per artifact. Damage mills the Horde; empty its library and kill its creatures. Listed under Horde.
Defeat a God Journey into Nyx (2014) Xenagos and his Revelers 1+ Xenagos can't leave the battlefield while any Reveler remains. Clear the Revelers, then take him down.

Rules all three share

  • Regular Magic rules apply, except where the deck's sheet says otherwise.
  • You make the choices for the challenge deck. Wizards' guidance for odd interactions was to choose whatever "feels fun" and intuitive (Hero's Path FAQ, as quoted by a Wizards forum representative).
  • Ignore effects that would make the challenge deck draw or discard, or do anything impossible.
  • A challenge permanent that would go anywhere other than its library or graveyard goes to its graveyard instead.
  • The challenge cards have no mana cost. The deck casts them for free, as spells that can be responded to.
  • Optional Hero cards start on your battlefield and help you (see below).

Hero cards

Hero cards came from Wizards' Hero's Path, a campaign of quests across the Theros block that awarded Heroes for completing quests, including beating the Challenge Decks. A Hero sits on the battlefield from the start of the game, like a Vanguard card. Face the Hydra allows up to two Heroes; Battle the Horde and Defeat a God allow up to three different Heroes. You never need one.

Hero From Text
The Protector Theros {T}: Prevent the next 1 damage that would be dealt to any target this turn.
The Philosopher Theros {2}, {T}: Tap target creature.
The Avenger Theros {3}, {T}: Target creature you control gains deathtouch until end of turn.
The Warrior Theros {T}: Target creature you control gains haste until end of turn.
The Hunter Theros {T}: Target creature you control gets +1/+1 until end of turn.
The Harvester Theros {T}: Draw a card, then discard a card.
The Slayer Theros You start the game with an additional 7 life.
The General Born of the Gods Exile The General: Creatures you control get +1/+1 until end of turn. Untap them.
The Savant Born of the Gods Exile The Savant: Tap all creatures your opponents control. Those creatures don't untap during their controllers' next untap steps.
The Tyrant Born of the Gods Exile The Tyrant: Creatures your opponents control get -1/-1 until end of turn.
The Warmonger Born of the Gods Exile The Warmonger: Creatures you control get +2/+0 and gain haste until end of turn.
The Provider Born of the Gods Exile The Provider: Put two +1/+1 counters on target creature you control. You gain life equal to that creature's toughness.
The Explorer Born of the Gods You may play an additional land on each of your turns.
The Vanquisher Born of the Gods Your starting hand size is increased by one. Your maximum hand size is increased by one.
The Destined Journey into Nyx Spells you control that target a creature you control cost {2} less to cast.
The Champion Journey into Nyx {2}, {T}, Exile The Champion: Search your library for a legendary artifact card that isn't a creature, reveal it, and put it onto the battlefield. Then shuffle your library.

Scryfall lists the first five Theros Heroes, and the first five Born of the Gods Heroes, as versions of a single card number (1a–1e and 4a–4e), which suggests each was one card with five Heroes to choose from. Journey into Nyx also had Hero Artifact Equipment and Enhancement Stickers, used to forge a personal "Godslayer" weapon for the final quest. Those are described on the Defeat a God page.

Getting the decks

All three are out of print since 2014. Sealed copies still turn up from secondhand game retailers and marketplaces. Search by the exact product name: Theros Challenge Deck: Face the Hydra, Born of the Gods Challenge Deck: Battle the Horde, and Journey into Nyx Challenge Deck: Defeat a God. MTG Wiki gives Face the Hydra's original price as $11.99. Prices and stock change; check listings before buying.

Each sealed deck should contain the 60-card challenge deck and a paper copy of the rules playmat. If you can't find one, every rules page here lists the full decklist and card text, so you can proxy the deck. Use distinct sleeves or card backs for the challenge deck so it can't mix with player decks.

Hero cards were promotional Game Day and Hero's Path items and are harder to find; proxy them from the table above if needed.

As a boxed set

A game-night box built on one of these decks needs the challenge deck, its rules sheet, tokens and counters (Face the Hydra needs damage tracking for Heads; Defeat a God needs a way to track many Revelers), and, optionally, Hero cards. Record any house rules, such as a chosen multiplayer turn structure, against the exact rules page.

  • Card text: Scryfall sets Face the Hydra, Battle the Horde, Defeat a God, and Hero's Path (THS, BNG, JOU).
