Defeat a God

Rules · Wizards' Journey into Nyx Challenge Deck (2014)

Xenagos has ascended to godhood, and his revel is spreading. While any of his Revelers remain, he is untouchable. Strip away his followers, then defeat the god. It was the Journey into Nyx Game Day challenge and the final quest of the Hero's Path.

Players and materials

  • One player, or any number of players with the group play rules.
  • The 60-card Xenagos deck below. The originals have indigo special card backs.
  • A Magic deck for each player. The sheet does not set a deck size or card pool. Wizards' Hero's Path FAQ says any Magic deck will work and that the Challenge Decks are tuned against Standard decks; the Game Day article asked players to bring a sixty-card deck.
  • Optionally up to three different Hero cards (see Hero cards). Each Hero card may be used only once against this deck.

Setup

  1. You may put up to three different Hero cards onto the battlefield. You don't need a Hero.
  2. Put Xenagos Ascended and two Rollicking Throng onto the battlefield. Their "enters the battlefield" abilities don't trigger as the game begins.
  3. Shuffle the rest of the deck to form Xenagos's library.
  4. You start at 20 life, shuffle, and draw an opening hand.

The sheet does not say who takes the first turn. Agree before play.

Your turns

  • You attack Xenagos Ascended and his Revelers directly with your creatures. Any number of creatures can attack a single Reveler.
  • You may target Xenagos as though he were a player.
  • Xenagos Ascended can't leave the battlefield as long as any Reveler is on the battlefield. Destroy, bounce or exile effects do nothing to him until the Revelers are gone.
  • Many Revelers have a Hero's Reward that triggers when they leave the battlefield.

Xenagos's turn

  1. At the beginning of Xenagos's first main phase, reveal the top two cards of his library. Xenagos casts them. You choose the order.
  2. Xenagos Ascended and the Revelers don't attack unless a card says they do. Ecstatic Piper, Maddened Oread, Impulsive Charge, Xenagos's Scorn and Dance of Panic make them attack.

Xenagos rules

  • You make the choice whenever Xenagos needs to make a decision.
  • Ignore effects that would make Xenagos draw or discard, or do anything impossible.
  • If one of Xenagos's permanents would move to any zone other than his library or graveyard, put it into his graveyard instead.

Winning and losing

  • You win if Xenagos Ascended leaves the battlefield. That can only happen when no Revelers are on the battlefield. (His card also reads: "When Xenagos Ascended leaves the battlefield, each player wins the game.")
  • You lose if you have 0 or less life, or if you have to draw from an empty library.

Difficulty

  • Harder: start with three Rollicking Throng on the battlefield.
  • Easier: start with one.

Rulings

These come from a Wizards forum representative's answers in May 2014, quoting the Hero's Path FAQ: "If you have an intuitive sense of how to resolve that interaction that feels fun, do that!"

  • The two starting Rollicking Throngs don't trigger.
  • When two cards are revealed, the player deciding for Xenagos chooses the order they are cast in.
  • When Xenagos's Scorn gives him trample, the player deciding for him should play to win and assign excess damage to you.
  • Impulsive Charge creates a delayed trigger at the beginning of combat. It affects Revelers that entered after the Charge resolved.

Group play

Wizards published the multiplayer rules for Game Day, as given by the deck's developer, Sam Stoddard:

  • Follow the rules printed on the playmat.
  • All players facing Xenagos share one life total of 20.
  • For each player beyond the first, the Xenagos deck starts with one additional Rollicking Throng on the battlefield.

Any number of players can play together.

MTG Wiki describes Challenge Decks differently

MTG Wiki's general Challenge Deck page says players each have 20 life and take their turns simultaneously, and that the deck wins only when all players are eliminated. For this deck, Wizards' article and FAQ give a shared total of 20.

The Godslayer

The Hero's Path ended by forging a Godslayer to use against Xenagos. Its Journey into Nyx Hero's Path items were five Hero Artifact Equipment cards and a sheet of Enhancement Stickers.

The weapon is the Hero card from quest 7, the Journey into Nyx Prerelease. Unlike other Hero cards it is an Equipment: it starts on the battlefield and you equip it to your creatures as you would any other Equipment.

Forging happened at the Prerelease. After each round, both players could take a component sticker, the match winner choosing first. Three component stickers were redeemed for one enhancement sticker, which gives the weapon a permanent new ability. There were seven enhancements, each made from a different combination of components.

This is the card text on Scryfall.

Equipment Text
Spear of the General Equipped creature gets +2/+0 and has first strike. Equip {2}
Cloak of the Philosopher Equipped creature gets +1/+1. {2}: Untap equipped creature. Equip {2}
Lash of the Tyrant Equipped creature gets +1/+2 and has deathtouch. Equip {2}
Axe of the Warmonger Equipped creature gets +2/+1 and has haste. Equip {2}
Bow of the Hunter Equipped creature has "{T}: This creature deals 2 damage to any target." Equip {2}

Enhancement Stickers options: vigilance and can block an additional creature each combat; whenever equipped creature deals damage, put a +1/+1 counter on it; whenever it deals damage, scry 1; protection from enchantments; double strike; whenever it deals damage, create a 1/1 white Soldier token; whenever it deals damage, untap two target lands.

Qty Card Stats Text

4 Pheres-Band Revelers Centaur Reveler, 4/4 Hero's Reward — When it leaves the battlefield, each player draws a card.
2 Maddened Oread Enchantment Creature — Nymph Reveler, 4/2 As long as five or more Revelers are on the battlefield, Maddened Oread attacks each turn if able. Hero's Reward — When it leaves the battlefield, each player gains 3 life.
2 Serpent Dancers Human Reveler, 2/4 Deathtouch. Hero's Reward — When it leaves the battlefield, each player draws a card.
2 Wild Maenads Human Reveler, 3/1 First strike. Hero's Reward — When it leaves the battlefield, each player gains 3 life.

Two of the six Rollicking Throngs start on the battlefield in a normal game.

Sorceries (23)

Qty Card Text
7 Impulsive Charge At the beginning of combat this turn, all Revelers gain haste until end of turn and attack this combat if able.
5 Xenagos's Strike Xenagos's Strike deals 4 damage to each player.
4 Impulsive Return Return two cards named Ecstatic Piper from Xenagos's graveyard to the battlefield. At the beginning of combat this turn, Impulsive Return deals damage to each player equal to the number of Revelers on the battlefield.
3 Xenagos's Scorn Xenagos Ascended gains trample until end of turn and attacks this turn if able.
2 Impulsive Destruction Each player may sacrifice an artifact or enchantment. Impulsive Destruction deals 3 damage to each player who didn't sacrifice a permanent this way.
2 Rip to Pieces At the beginning of combat this turn, each Reveler deals 1 damage to each player and each creature those players control.

Enchantments (4)

Qty Card Text
2 Dance of Flame Whenever a Reveler attacks, Dance of Flame deals 1 damage to each player.
2 Dance of Panic As long as five or more Revelers are on the battlefield, all Revelers have haste and attack each turn if able.

Open questions

Targeting Xenagos as a player: the sheet lets you target Xenagos "as though he were a player," but does not say what damage or life loss dealt to him that way does. He is also a 6/5 creature. Agree before play; a natural reading is to treat it as damage to the creature.

  • Group play, turns: do the players take one shared turn, or separate turns? Wizards' group-play rules do not say.
  • Group play, Xenagos's choices: with several players, who decides for Xenagos?
  • Group play, Heroes: does each player get three Hero cards, or does the group share three?
