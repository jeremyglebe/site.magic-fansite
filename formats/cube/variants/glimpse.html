Glimpse Draft

Rules · costofanarchy's draft, from the Cube Draft Format Primer (thesidestepkids, December 2016)

Each pick, draft one card and discard two.

Players and materials

  • 2–8 players.
  • 135 cube cards per player, a basic land supply, and the cube's tokens.
  • Expected pool: 45 cards each.

The primer's "cards seen" figure does not match its procedure

The primer says each player sees 243 cards in an 8-player draft. By the procedure below, a player handles packs of 15, 12, 9, 6 and 3 cards in each round: 45 cards a round, and 405 over nine rounds. 243 is what one player sees in a two-player draft, where the same two packs go back and forth (27 different cards a round).

Prepare the draft

  1. Shuffle the cards and make nine packs of 15 cards for each player.
  2. Seat players in a circle.

Draft a round

  1. Each player takes one pack.
  2. Each player drafts one card from the pack in front of them.
  3. Each player then discards two cards from that pack, removing them from the draft.
  4. Each player passes the pack to their left.
  5. Repeat steps 2–4 until the packs are completely drafted or discarded. Each player drafts 5 cards and discards 10 per round.

Between rounds

Repeat for all 9 packs, alternating the direction players pass each round.

Variant: burn one

Glimpse is also played as pick one, discard one ("burn-1"), as described by Riptide Lab posters. The primer's version, above, is burn-2. Announce which one before the draft; burn-1 needs fewer packs for the same pool.

Build decks

The primer this draft comes from covers drafting only. After the draft, use the Booster-draft Cube deck and play rules:

  • Build a deck of at least 40 cards from your drafted pool. (The primer says each player builds "40-card decks" from their pool and unlimited basic lands.)
  • Add ordinary basic lands from the land supply as needed.
  • Only cards you drafted are available. Cards discarded during the draft, and cards nobody drafted, are out of play for this event.
  • Your drafted cards that are not in the main deck form your sideboard. You may change the deck between games using that sideboard and the basic lands, keeping at least 40 cards.

Play games

Choose pairings and a match structure before play. In normal two-player games, each player starts at 20 life, draws seven cards, and uses normal mulligans. Determine who chooses to play or draw normally, unless the draft's rules above say who chooses; the player taking the first turn skips their first draw step. Play ordinary Magic turns with normal victory and loss conditions.

Reset the cube

Return every drafted and discarded card to the cube, separate the borrowed basic lands, and collect tokens and other components.

Open questions

  • Discards: none of the reachable sources says whether discarded cards are shown or kept face down. Keep them face down unless the group agrees otherwise.
  • Source wording: the primer's step 4 says "until both packs are entirely drafted/discarded," which reads like a leftover from a two-player version. This page takes it to mean the packs in the current round.

Sources

  • Procedure from the Cube Draft Format Primer source notes, which credits costofanarchy and links the MTGSalvation thread (unreachable; see the Cube draft research notes) Glimpse / Burnfour draft thread.
