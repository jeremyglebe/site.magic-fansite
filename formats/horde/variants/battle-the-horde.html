Battle the Horde (Wizards Challenge Deck)

Rules · Wizards of the Coast's 2014 Minotaur challenge deck

Battle the Horde was a Wizards of the Coast Challenge Deck for Born of the Gods (2014), used at that set's Game Day and sold as a boxed product. It is a 60-card, self-running Minotaur deck with its own rules sheet. It was designed for one player, and Wizards later published a multiplayer option.

This is Wizards' own ruleset and differs from fan Horde versions. You make the Horde's decisions (not random), the Horde reveals a fixed two cards per turn plus one per artifact, and it has no tokens. Do not mix it with Classic Zombie Horde rules without announcing it.

Players and materials

  • 1 player, or more with the multiplayer option.
  • The 60-card Horde deck below. The original cards have special red card backs and no mana costs. To build it now, find the original product or proxy the cards from the text below.
  • A Magic deck for each player. The rules sheet does not set a deck size or card pool; agree before play.
  • Optional: up to three different Hero cards per player (see Hero cards).

Setup

  1. Shuffle the Horde deck to form the Horde's library. It has no hand and no life total.
  2. Each player may put up to three different Hero cards onto the battlefield. You don't need a Hero to play.
  3. Shuffle your own deck, draw an opening hand, and mulligan normally. You start at 20 life.
  4. You take three turns before the Horde takes its first turn.

Your turns

Play normal Magic turns, with these additions:

  • You can attack the Horde with your creatures as though it were a player, even before it has taken a turn.
  • You can target the Horde with spells and abilities as though it were a player, even before it has taken a turn.
  • The Horde has no life total. If the Horde would lose life, put that many cards from the top of its library into its graveyard instead. (Combat damage to the Horde makes it lose life, so it mills too.)

The Horde's turns

The Horde takes normal Magic turns (untapping, combat and so on), with these changes:

  1. At the beginning of the Horde's precombat main phase, reveal the top two cards of its library. The Horde casts those cards.
  2. Each Horde artifact on the battlefield makes the Horde reveal and cast one more card at that time, starting the turn after the artifact enters.
  3. The Minotaurs have haste or enter tapped as printed, and attack each turn if able.

Horde rules

  • You make the choice whenever the Horde needs to make a decision.
  • Ignore effects that would make the Horde draw or discard cards, or do anything else impossible.
  • If one of the Horde's permanents would move to any zone other than its library or graveyard, put it into the Horde's graveyard instead. (So bouncing or exiling a Horde permanent sends it to the graveyard.)

Hero's rewards

Each Horde artifact has a Hero's Reward that triggers when it is put into a graveyard from anywhere, including when you mill it from the library. Watch for them when you damage the Horde.

Winning and losing

  • You win when the Horde has no cards left in its library and controls no creatures.
  • You lose if you have 0 or less life, or if you have to draw from an empty library.

Difficulty

  • Harder: take only two turns before the Horde's first turn.
  • Easier: take four turns.

Multiplayer option

Wizards first said the deck could not be played multiplayer, then published multiplayer rules in "Battle The Horde With Your Friends!" (February 28, 2014):

  • Follow the rules printed on the playmat.
  • All players share one life total of 20.
  • The Horde flips one additional card each turn for each additional player. Two players face three cards a turn.

The article sets no maximum number of players. MTG Wiki recommends up to four.

The Horde deck (60 cards)

The challenge cards have no mana cost; the Horde casts them for free.

Artifacts (7)

Each artifact also reads: "At the beginning of the Horde's first main phase, reveal an additional card from the top of the Horde's library. The Horde casts that card."

Unquenchable Fury here is a challenge card, not the later Commander card with the same name.

Hero cards

Hero cards came from Wizards' Hero's Path promotion, a campaign of quests across the Theros block. They sit on the battlefield from the start of the game, like Vanguard cards. On the Hero's Path, a player could bring up to three Heroes from earlier quests, using each Hero once, and earned a new Hero by defeating the Horde (MTG Wiki). For a one-off game, simply choose up to three different Heroes.

These are the Theros and Born of the Gods Hero's Path cards on Scryfall. Journey into Nyx added more Heroes after this challenge.

Related challenge decks

Wizards made two more Theros-block Challenge Decks, Face the Hydra (Theros) and Defeat a God (Journey into Nyx). They are not Horde formats and have their own rules. All three, the full Hero card list, and buying notes are on the Challenge Decks page.

Open questions

  • Turn structure: in a multiplayer game, do the players take one shared turn or separate turns? Wizards' Challenge Deck description says players "take their turn simultaneously" (MTG Wiki), which suggests a shared turn.
  • Who decides for the Horde: the single-player rule says "you" make the Horde's choices. With several players, who makes them?
  • Hero cards: does each player get up to three?
