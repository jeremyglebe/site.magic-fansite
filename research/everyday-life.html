Everyday life and ordinary New York

A card catalogue for boxes that make mundane life the main attraction: residents, pigeons, pets, lunch, newspapers, rent, homework, shops, tools, and public transportation. Marvel and Spider-Man are the starting point; other Magic settings and crossovers supply additional candidates. This page explains the catalogue and how to use it; the card-by-card tables are on the linked category pages. The New York Animal Invasion draft box uses it as starting material.

Researched October 19, 2026

A card belongs here because of what it represents. An ordinary person's ability can draw cards or grant counters without making that person a superhero, and School Daze qualifies through its homework premise even though it also has a Fight Crime mode. Every card keeps its printed rules, creature types, and format restrictions.

This snapshot contains 524 individually catalogued cards: 97 Marvel entries and 427 additional candidates. The fit labels comprise 125 Direct, 299 Setting, and 100 Almost entries. Ordinary-animal token examples are listed separately.

Browse the catalogue

Food, objects, and transportation Meals, tools, household objects, recreation, and vehicles.
Places and everyday activities Streets, businesses, homes, errands, bills, school, social events, and ordinary crime. Includes instants, sorceries, enchantments, and lands.
Almost matches Explicitly qualified exceptions such as Venomized Cat, supernatural pets, disguised heroes, animated household objects, and silver-bordered jokes.
Ordinary-animal tokens Cards literally named Cat, Dog, Rat, Bird, Mouse, Squirrel, and Bear, with their specific token characteristics.

Download the complete catalogue as CSV for spreadsheet sorting, or the structured JSON for project tools. Both contain the same entries as the category pages: exact card names, types, set and collector identifiers, mana costs, colors, fit judgments, notes, and Scryfall links. The token examples have their own page and are not in the downloads.

How the fit labels work

Label Meaning
Direct A strong ordinary-life premise that can fit the city joke: a person at work, a pet, a meal, a real object, or an everyday activity. A superhero can appear in the surrounding story without disqualifying the ordinary subject.
Setting The person, animal, object, or activity is ordinary, but the era, habitat, species' real-world range, artwork, or franchise is less suited to present-day New York. A bear belongs here because it is ordinary wildlife, not normal subway wildlife.
Almost Something specifically extraordinary is part of the subject: powers, a transformation, an alien infection, an animated object, an anthropomorphic fantasy species, or special joke-card rules. Every such row explains the exception.

The labels are our editorial judgments, not Scryfall categories or legality labels. Some borderline choices may move after looking at the cards together. Venomized Cat is deliberately included as an Almost: the joke of a little cat that caught alien goo survives, and the entry states its Symbiote Cat Villain identity.

Starting points

For the ordinary New York idea, begin with City Pigeon, Aunt May, J. Jonah Jameson, Gallant Citizen, Selfless Police Captain, Daily Bugle Reporters, Supportive Parents, Common Crook, Taxi Driver, and Willie Lumpkin, Postman. The noncreature side is just as central: Bagel and Schmear, Hot Dog Cart, Daily Bugle Newspaper, Peter Parker's Camera, News Helicopter, Subway Train, Passenger Ferry, Rent Is Due, School Daze, and Urban Retreat.

Outside Marvel, Courier of Comestibles, Action News Crew, Skateboard, Manhole Cover, Big Apple, 3 a.m., and the TMNT pizza cards bring useful modern-city material. Dog Walker, Citizen's Crowbar, Jackhammer, Tramway Station, and Getaway Car offer strong ordinary roles and objects from fantasy cities. Sanctuary Cat, Helpful Hunter, Warehouse Tabby, Selfless Savior, Carrier Pigeons, Sewer Rats, and Brood of Cockroaches make the animal side substantial enough to explore without turning it into a collection of superheroes.

These names are entry points, not a proposed decklist. The category pages hold the much larger card-by-card catalogue.

Rules distinctions that matter when building

News Helicopter is an artifact creature — Construct. It is not a Vehicle, despite depicting a helicopter. Subway Train and Passenger Ferry are Vehicles and are not creature cards in the deck. J. Jonah Jameson and Aunt May are Human Citizens, Selfless Police Captain is a Human Detective, and City Pigeon is a Bird. A shared city theme does not make them share a creature type.

For Creature Feature, count the actual types and the complete deck-construction requirements. Human could connect many of the people, with birds or other non-Humans using the permitted support space; Citizen is a narrower choice. Residents are not all Citizens, and animals are not all one type.

Modal cards keep every mode and double-faced cards keep both faces. Including School Daze for Do Homework does not remove Fight Crime. A box that changes a rule must document the change against its chosen base format. The same care applies to Horde targeting, optional abilities, repeat activations, and effects that require a player to make decisions.

We usually prefer distinct nonbasic cards. Some candidates reward repeated names, such as Gathering Throng, Squadron Hawk, Pack Mastiff, and Relentless Rats. Their notes say so; a box is not committed to duplicates because a card is catalogued. Basic lands and token quantities follow the selected game's rules and actual component needs.

Coverage and research method

This is a best-effort discovery catalogue; it does not contain every mundane image ever printed. No single query finds this theme. Names alone miss ordinary scenes on abstractly named spells, while a Human or Cat type alone does not prove that a character is unpowered or an animal is mundane.

The search combined:

  1. A broad pass through the released Marvel-art card pool, including SPM, SPE, MSH, MSC, MAR, and Marvel Secret Lair material. See the separate Marvel set reference for the complete code inventory.
  2. The TMNT card pools and targeted passes through New Capenna, Karlov Manor, Neon Dynasty, Fallout, and Doctor Who for city life, civilians, and contemporary objects.
  3. Cross-set creature searches for Citizens, ordinary pets, birds, rats, squirrels, bears, other animals, and common insect/pest names. Obvious anthropomorphic heroes, spirits, and monsters were excluded or moved to Almost.
  4. Food and Vehicle searches, plus grouped name searches for occupations, household tools, transport, places, money problems, meals, school, leisure, maintenance, and crime. This explicitly included noncreature cards.
  5. Exact-name checks, Oracle-type and rules-text review, and selected artwork checks for ambiguous candidates. Each row links a specific printing so that visual fit can be reviewed before selecting the physical cards. The catalogue does not claim that every alternate artwork was inspected.

Card facts were retrieved through the project's Scryfall MCP server. Searches used a release cutoff of date<=2026-10-19; token and art-card records were separated from the ordinary-card list. Large result sets were divided into smaller queries rather than treating the first result page as the entire pool.

The saved search log records the successful search families and their scopes so the searches can be repeated. Its results are uncurated and overlap, so a record count is not a count of recommended cards.

Useful repeatable searches

Discovery task Scryfall search
All Marvel artwork Marvel card pool
Marvel's people outside the obvious Hero/Villain types Human creatures without those two types — also inspect Heroes/Villains manually; Night Nurse and Common Crook show why this filter is incomplete.
Citizen creatures across Magic Citizens — includes nonhuman and transformed residents.
Cats, dogs, rats, and mice Pet and pest types — includes monsters and anthropomorphic characters to screen out.
Actual Food objects Food cards — some are living Food creatures or magical meals.
Transportation Vehicles — also search names; News Helicopter demonstrates why Vehicle alone is incomplete.
Literally Cat Cat tokens — a token search, not permission to put tokens into an ordinary deck.

When extending the list, record the full card name, a particular printing, the actual type line, the fit label, and the reason. Keep the explanation on every almost match. A named character's civilian job, an alternate artwork, or an ordinary-sounding title can be worth considering as long as the entry states the extraordinary part.
