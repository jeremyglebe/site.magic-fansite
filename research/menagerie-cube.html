Menagerie Cube: candidate pool

Every card considered for the Menagerie Cube, with its current status and, for cut cards, the reason. Use it to check why a card is in or out, to find replacements when the list changes, and to see how the current 270 slots are balanced. The cube's rules and its card list by theme are on the boxed-set page.

Card data from Scryfall, November 15, 2026 · 270 draftable slots selected from 374 candidates

The pool began as the owner's Archidekt idea list, "All Mutate, Bestow, Emerge, Augment, Champion, ATLA Hybrid Animals, similar & support". It is a list of cards that looked interesting, not a cube list or a purchase list. Cut cards stay in the tables with their reason.

Files

  • Source list: archidekt-source-list.txt, as pasted on November 15, 2026. The Archidekt deck is private. Its quantities are the Archidekt default of four and mean nothing for the cube.
  • Card snapshot: cards.json. Oracle text, types, costs, mechanic tags, source, and notes for each candidate, from Scryfall's /cards/collection endpoint on November 15, 2026. Bat- is the Unfinity card; Rhino- is the Unstable card.
  • Archidekt import of the current candidates: archidekt-import.txt; its format is described under Full candidate pool.

Pool status values

  • candidate: may go in the cube. Not yet reviewed.
  • core: shared fixing: the Vivid lands.
  • cut: ...: cut, with the reason. Cut cards stay in the table so the list matches its sources.
  • keep (owner): the owner wants it in the cube.
  • proposed (shortlist): suggested to fill open slots; not approved yet.

Full candidate pool

Grouped by the color of the card's front face. Lands include land creatures and lands with meld or transform. Modal double-faced cards with a land back face are listed by their front face. The Source column separates the original idea list from the interaction search.

An Archidekt import of every current candidate is in archidekt-import.txt. It uses Archidekt's export format, 1x Name (set) 123 [Category] ^Label,#color^. Categories group cards by mechanic (Build-Around, Augment, Host, Mutate, Bestow, Meld, Stuck Together, Hybrid Animal, Plain Animal, Interaction, Fixing, Other). Labels mark status: Keep, or Safe to cut. Cards marked cut are left out. S.N.O.T. is a stack of four, and each Vivid land has four copies. B.F.M. is left out because Archidekt's importer can't match its name; add both halves by hand.

Interaction candidates

This section records the first interaction search: 50 cards found by Scryfall search on November 15, 2026, to give each color answers to creatures. Most come from the sets the theme is drawn from: Ikoria (mutate), Theros (bestow), Avatar: The Last Airbender, Unstable, and Unfinity. Many of these cards have since been cut; the full candidate pool has each card's current status, and Interaction by color lists the interaction now in the cube.

Removal that scales with power (bites, fights, sacrifice-and-fling) and edicts fit this cube better than flat destroy effects. A combined or mutated creature holds two or more cards, so one destroy spell trades for all of them.
