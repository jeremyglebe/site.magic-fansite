New York Animal Invasion card candidates

Checked November 24, 2026

Candidate lists for both sides of New York Animal Invasion: the New Yorkers pool the survivors draft from, and the animal deck they fight. Nothing here is approved. The lists exist to be cut.

Every card comes from the everyday-life card catalogue and was picked by the rules on the box page: present-day New York only, repeat copies allowed, city animals only, and a few near misses picked for the joke. Card text, sizes, and prices are from Scryfall on the date above. The machine-readable list is candidates.json.

New Yorkers pool

224 cards, 98 different. The box rule asks for about 220 cards, the number a four-player draft deals. Copies were set by one rule, as a starting point:

  • Legendary cards, rares, and mythics: 1 copy. A named person appears once.
  • Common creatures: 4 copies.
  • Other commons: 3 copies.
  • Uncommons: 2 copies.
  • Lands: 2 copies of a common, 1 of anything else.
Group Different cards Copies
White 22 50
Blue 9 19
Black 4 15
Red 16 39
Green 15 35
Multicolor 5 12
Colorless 18 40
Land 9 14
Total 98 224

119 of the 224 cards are creatures. At each card's listed printing the pool costs about $58.

What stands out in the pool

  • Black is nearly empty. Four different black cards qualify: three kinds of criminal and a pizza. The rats and cockroaches took the color. Survivor decks will be white, red, and green, with some blue.
  • Blue is thin. Nine different cards, mostly scientists and reporters.
  • Pets are on this side. Six dogs and cats that help their owner are in the pool. Strays and vermin are in the animal deck.
  • Removal is scarce. The cards that destroy or damage a creature are Grounded for Life, Anchovy & Banana Pizza, Spicy Oatmeal Pizza, Steel Wrecking Ball, Chainsaw, and Wisecrack.

Animal deck

100 cards: 60 weak animal cards and tokens, and 40 other cards. That is the base's mix, about 60 token cards and 40 others. The base uses 45, 60, 75, or 100 of them for one to four survivors.

The printed cards cost about $65, most of it in Rat Colony and Relentless Rats. Tokens are extra.

Spells

12 cards. A spell also stops the wave. Each one passed two tests: the Horde never has to pick anything, and the effect is worth something to a deck with unlimited mana, no hand, and no life total.

Copies Card Cost Type For the Horde
2 Release the Dogs {3}{W} Sorcery Four 1/1 white Dogs. The art is a pack of dogs charging out through a gate.
2 Migratory Route {3}{W}{U} Sorcery Four 1/1 white Birds with flying. Landcycling never comes up: the Horde casts the card when it is revealed.
2 Chatterstorm {1}{G} Sorcery One Squirrel, copied for each spell cast before it this turn. Every card in the wave counts, tokens included, because the Horde casts them.
3 Gnawing Crescendo {2}{R} Instant Every Horde creature gets +2/+0 this turn, and each nontoken one that dies leaves a Rat behind.
1 Swarmyard Massacre {3}{B}{B} Sorcery Two Squirrels, then every creature that is not an Insect, Rat, Spider, or Squirrel shrinks by the number of those the Horde controls. It hits the Horde's own cats, dogs, and birds too. The art shows a squirrelfolk, not an ordinary squirrel.
2 Revenge of the Rats {2}{B}{B} Sorcery A tapped Rat for each creature card in the Horde's graveyard, so it grows as the survivors mill the Horde. Under NY4 the Horde casts it once more from its graveyard, including when damage mills it there.

The spells make tokens that are not in the deck. Keep a supply beside it: white Dogs, white Birds with flying, green Squirrels, and black Rats that can't block.

What stands out in the deck

  • Rats feed each other. Rat Colony, Swarm of Rats, and Pestilence Rats all count Rats, and the deck holds 6 Rat tokens and 26 Rat cards. Late in a game one Rat Colony can be very large. Untested.
  • Three cards need a random choice. Drainpipe Vermin, Gnawing Vermin, and Rambunctious Mutt each pick a target, eight copies in all. The base has the Horde choose at random. Cut them if nobody should have to roll for the Horde.
  • Cockroaches do not stay dead. Brood of Cockroaches and Endless Cockroaches return to the Horde's hand when they die. The base has the Horde cast a permanent returned to its hand again on its next turn, and this list treats these the same way. The survivors can still win: a card in the Horde's hand is not on the battlefield.
  • Three near misses are in: Venomized Cat, Giant Cockroach, and Undercity Dire Rat.
  • Most of the deck still has 1 power. The original Horde's tokens are 55 Zombies at 2/2 and 5 Zombie Giants. Whether this Horde threatens a drafted deck is untested.

Rat printings

Rats count as city animals whatever the art shows. Where a card has more than one artwork, some are closer to an ordinary rat than others. These notes come from looking at the artworks at thumbnail size on Scryfall.

Card Artworks Suggested printing Why
Rat Colony 3 DOM 101 Ordinary rats in wreckage. Secret Lair 36 also works: white rats among paper lanterns. The Special Guests art is a rat in armor.
Relentless Rats 10 A25 105 or 5DN 58 Both show a mass of ordinary rats. The eight Secret Lair and Special Guests artworks are stylized, and one shows a cloaked figure.
Typhoid Rats 2 ISD 120 Rats indoors by a window. The FRF art works too.
Swarm of Rats 1 any One artwork.
Pestilence Rats 1 any One artwork.

More rats, if the deck wants them: Plague Rats, Ruin Rat, Voracious Vermin, Tangled Colony, Muck Rats, Bog Rats, Ravenous Rats. Ravenous Rats has three artworks; the Urza's Destiny one shows rats among books.

Questions the lists raise

  • Do other city animals count when the art shows a fantasy setting? Rats do. The catalogue also has Dakmor Bat and Kelinore Bat, Vexing Gull, Swamp Mosquito, Killer Bees, Fire Ants, and Cheeky House-Mouse.
  • What happens when the Horde would draw a card? The base does not say. This list treats a draw as doing nothing.
  • Do token cards in the Horde's graveyard count as creature cards for Revenge of the Rats?
  • Are pets on the survivors' side and strays on the Horde's?
  • Is a pool with almost no black acceptable, or do the criminals need company?
  • Does Boarded Window stay? It takes 1 power off every attacker, and most of the Horde has 1 power.

Not checked

  • No game has been played with either list.
  • Prices are for the printing linked, not the cheapest printing.
  • Token printings other than the examples named.
  • Artworks were judged at thumbnail size.

Forge

Every card on both lists is in the pinned Forge version (2.0.16). The check was by card name against Forge's card files.
