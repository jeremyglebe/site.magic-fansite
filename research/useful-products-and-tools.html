Products and tools

Containers and tools for assembling, storing, and reusing game-night boxes. Capacities are the maker's or seller's claims unless a line says otherwise. We have not fit-tested any of them with sleeved, stickered packets, so check a fit with the card storage calculator before buying in quantity.

Listings as of October 21, 2026. Stock, prices, and listings change. Match the size and the product identifier.

Need Product
Reusable booster containers for a cube draft Dragon Shield Cube Shells or Cubeamajigs
Boxes for Jumpstart packets Burger Tokens Perfect Fit Deckboxes, our pick. APACALI shells and slim Jumpstart boxes are alternatives.
Removing trapped air from sleeved packets The Sturdy Deck Press
Checking a fit before buying Card storage calculator

Cube booster containers

Dragon Shield Cube Shells

Reusable booster containers for cube drafts. Dragon Shield sells eight shells per pack, each holding fifteen double-sleeved cards or twenty single-sleeved cards. They close securely and have a writing field on top for labels.

  • Manufacturer's product page and Amazon listing, Blood Red, eight-pack
  • Identifiers: ASIN B09W6CQPHH; model AT-30550; Dragon Shield Cube Shell, Blood Red.
  • How many: an eight-player booster-draft cube with three packs per player needs twenty-four shells, which is three eight-packs.

Cubeamajigs decorative booster packs

Reusable cube packs with printed artwork. The Land Panorama Set has art by Daarken: five designs, two of each, for ten containers. Hit Point Press advertises 15 or more double-sleeved cards, or 20 or more single-sleeved cards, per container. The plastic packs have interlocking closures and a scratch-resistant coating.

  • Publisher's product page and Amazon listing, ten-pack. Other designs are in Hit Point Press's Cubeamajigs collection.
  • Identifiers: ASIN B0C1NGBT7W; Cubeamajigs Reusable Gaming Packs, Land Panorama Set.
  • How many: an eight-player draft needs twenty-four containers. Three ten-packs supply thirty, leaving six spares.

The advertised "up to 400 cards" is the ten-pack's combined unsleeved capacity, not a container's double-sleeved capacity.

Jumpstart packet boxes

A custom packet is twenty double-sleeved cards, plus a few extras if you add a title card, tokens, or a reference card. See Building and labeling custom packets.

Burger Tokens Perfect Fit Deckboxes

Our pick. Clear folding boxes sold in ten-packs, with published internal dimensions. The 37S leaves more room for tokens, reference cards, and double sleeves.

Size Internal dimensions Variant
30 sleeved (30S), ten-pack 67 × 95.5 × 18.5 mm 39339002167385
37 sleeved (37S), ten-pack 67 × 95.5 × 22.5 mm 21689594544217

The size names are not double-sleeved card counts. By depth, a 30S holds about 25 to 27 double-sleeved cards and a 37S about 31 to 33, with nothing left over for padding or for getting the cards out. Those figures use published sleeve thicknesses of 0.68 and 0.72 mm per double-sleeved card.

APACALI clear snap-tight card shells

An alternative. A twenty-four-pack of clear hinged cases, advertised as 3.8 × 2.7 × 0.82 inches with 1.2 mm walls.

  • Amazon listing
  • Identifiers: ASIN B09CH5VK1G; brand APACALI; 24-pack.

The listed size is the outside of the case. Subtracting the walls gives an interior of about 66.18 × 94.12 × 18.43 mm, which is 25 to 27 double-sleeved cards by depth. Width is the tighter fit: a 66 mm sleeve has 0.18 mm of clearance before molded corners or tolerances. The listing's own figures disagree with each other (its address says 0.94 inches and its description rounds to 0.8), so buy one pack and check before buying more.

Slim Jumpstart deck boxes

A compact alternative for a packet with no extras. Opaque latching boxes with interlocking sides and a card-removal slot, sold by jman2c on eBay. The seller advertises 20 or more double-sleeved, 25 or more single-sleeved, or 45 or more unsleeved cards, and gives no interior dimensions.

  • eBay listing, item 227289326606, in several colors and in quantities of 1, 3, 15, 24, or 36.
  • An Etsy listing from UsefulExtrusion appears to be the same product. Its seller says it holds twenty double-sleeved cards, with one or two more at a squeeze, and one buyer reported it too small.

It suits a plain twenty-card packet. It leaves no room for five more double-sleeved cards.

Tools

The Sturdy Deck Press

A 3D-printable press for squeezing trapped air out of sleeved or double-sleeved cards: MakerWorld model 1884468 by Nabes Teban. Cards load from the side, the upper tray attaches to the screw, and an optional lower tray helps with removal.

  • Print profile: the designer's profile 2018145 uses 0.2 mm layers, nine walls, and 33% infill. The original is printed in ABS, and the designer also recommends PETG.
  • Short stacks: the screw stops short of the base on purpose. For a twenty-card packet, add tokens, lands, or bulk cards under the stack.
  • Pressing does not give a fixed card thickness. Measure the pressed packet before using the storage calculator.

Card storage calculator

The card storage calculator works from a card stack or from a box. It handles single and double sleeves, pressed and post-play measurements, inserts, padding, and removal clearance, and it has presets for the Burger Tokens and APACALI boxes above.
